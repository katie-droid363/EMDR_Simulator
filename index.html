About EMDR Simulation

EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing) visual stimulation encourages bilateral brain activity by guiding the eyes horizontally back and forth.

How to use:
  • Sit comfortably with your head still, centered in front of the screen.
  • Follow the moving target smoothly with your eyes only.
  • Adjust speed and span width to a comfortable rhythm that doesn't cause eye strain.
  • Use stereo headphones if enabling Bilateral Audio for enhanced synchronization.

Note: This tool is intended for self-regulation, wellness, or certified clinical session support. It is not a substitute for professional mental health therapy.
